HOW TO PLAY

Super English World is an authentic retro platformer where you learn and master the Simple Present Tense in English with Mario power-ups and chiptune sound effects!

🎮 Controls:

  • Movement: Arrow Left / Right or A / D keys.
  • Jump: Arrow Up, W, or Spacebar.
  • Special Action / Ability: Key F or Shift (Fireballs, Iceballs, Hammers, Tail Attacks, Flight).
  • Crouch / Statue / Shell Slide: Arrow Down or S.

📚 Game Rules & Grammar:

  • Hit Mystery Question (?) Blocks to activate English Simple Present grammar challenges.
  • Answer correctly to receive instant Mario Power-Ups, Coins, Stars, and High Scores!
  • Defeat Goombas by stomping on them or using active projectile/power-up abilities.
  • Reach the Green Warp Pipe at the end of each stage to complete the level.
